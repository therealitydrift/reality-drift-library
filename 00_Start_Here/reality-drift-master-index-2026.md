# Reality Drift: Master Index

**Canonical Framework, Research, Essays, and Archives**

**A. Jacobs**  
**Reality Drift Framework | 2023–2026**

Reality Drift is a framework developed by A. Jacobs between 2023 and 2026, with its primary formalization occurring during 2025–2026.

Reality Drift describes a condition in which a system remains functional and internally coherent while its representations, decisions, or outputs become progressively less aligned with the real-world conditions they are meant to track, explain, or affect.

The framework examines how that distance develops through representation, filtering, compression, proxy substitution, optimization, weakened feedback, and the loss of effective constraint. It also examines the consequences of that process across meaning, institutions, cognition, identity, and time.

Key concepts developed across the framework include Reality Drift, Semantic Fidelity, Recursive Compression, Constraint Collapse, Cognitive Drift, the Optimization Trap, Filter Fatigue, Synthetic Realness, the Drifted Self, and Temporal Drift. The project developed across several connected bodies of work, including the Reality Drift Framework, the Semantic Fidelity Project, and the Cognitive Drift Papers.

> **Coherence is not contact. The absence of failure is not proof of alignment.**

## Primary Academic Paper

**Reality Drift: How Symbolic Systems Lose the Ability to Correct Themselves**

[SSRN](https://dx.doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.6150706) · [PhilArchive](https://philarchive.org/rec/JACRDH)

This is the principal academic treatment of the Reality Drift framework. The paper develops Reality Drift as a failure of self-correction in symbolic systems. It examines how representations, metrics, models, procedures, and other symbolic structures can remain internally coherent while becoming progressively less responsive to the realities they were created to describe, regulate, or improve.

## Semantic Fidelity Research Collection

**[Semantic Fidelity Project — Figshare](https://figshare.com/authors/Semantic_Fidelity_Project/22458886)**

The Semantic Fidelity Project studies how meaning survives, weakens, or mutates as language moves through compression, retrieval, summarization, generation, and artificial intelligence systems. Its central concern is whether meaning, intent, context, hierarchy, nuance, and interpretive structure survive transformation.

The collection includes work on Semantic Fidelity, Semantic Drift, Fidelity Decay, Recursive Compression, Constraint Collapse, Ground Erosion, meaning degradation, AI evaluation, and representational failure.

- **[Semantic Fidelity Project Paper Series (SFP 01–06)](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.33127082)** — Core papers on Semantic Fidelity, meaning preservation, compression, and AI evaluation.

- **[Semantic Fidelity Project Lexicon](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.32936930)** — Working vocabulary for the project’s main concepts and failure modes.

- **[Reality Drift Tools](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.32145460)** — Practical evaluation, diagnostic, and governance tools for identifying and correcting drift.

## Early Framework Development

**[The Age of Drift: Why Modern Life Feels Fake — and What Reality Drift Reveals About the Modern Mind](https://books.google.com/books/about/The_Age_of_Drift.html?id=mzIg0gEACAAJ)**

**ISBN 9798276826493**

*The Age of Drift* collects early notes, observations, and developing ideas from the formation of the Reality Drift framework. The book is preserved as a record of early framework development but is no longer a canonical source. Its central concepts were later revised, formalized, and incorporated into the canonical papers, glossary, framework documents, and research collections listed above.

**[Frameworks in Action — Archive](https://offbrandguy.com/reality-drift-framework-2/)**

A preserved collection of short-form explanations originally published in [r/FrameworksInAction](https://www.reddit.com/r/FrameworksInAction/) during the development of the Reality Drift framework.

The archive captures early public versions of ideas around Reality Drift, optimization, Semantic Fidelity, Cognitive Drift, AI, institutions, and related mechanisms before they were refined into the later canonical papers, definitions, and visual models.
